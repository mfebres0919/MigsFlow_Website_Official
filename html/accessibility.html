Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Our commitment

MigsFlow Web Design wants everyone to be able to use migsflow.com, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, screen magnification, voice control, or other assistive technology.

We work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA (opens in a new tab), the standard most widely used to measure website accessibility. Accessibility is ongoing work, and we review the site whenever we add or change pages.

What we've done

  • Semantic page structure with headings, landmarks, and a "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page.
  • Full keyboard support, with a visible focus outline that the fixed menu bar doesn't cover.
  • Descriptive link text, including notes when a link opens a new tab, your email app, or a PDF download.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images. Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Labeled form fields with clear error and success messages.
  • Color contrast that meets WCAG AA for text and controls.
  • Respect for your device's "reduce motion" setting.
  • Tagged, screen-reader-friendly PDFs for our Welcome Package and Homepage Redesign Packet, with a reading order, headings, and image descriptions.
  • Regular testing with automated tools (axe-core) and manual checks with a keyboard and a screen reader.

Known limitations

Some parts of the experience are provided by third-party services that we don't control, including:

  • HoneyBook (invoices and payments, processed by Stripe)
  • Netlify (form handling and hosting)
  • Links to outside sites, such as Google Maps, Instagram, TikTok, and client websites in our portfolio

We choose providers that publish their own accessibility commitments. If one of these tools gets in your way, contact us and we'll help you finish what you started another way, such as by email or phone.

Need help or found a problem?

If anything on this site is hard to use, or you need information in a different format, please let us know. Tell us the page and what happened, and we'll work with you to fix it.
