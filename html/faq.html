  • The timeline really depends on the project scope and how quickly you’re able to send feedback.

  • The buyout cost depends on what a comparable lump sum build would have been. Your monthly payments get subtracted from that total. For example, if the equivalent lump sum would be $1,500 and you've paid 6 months at $150/mo ($900), your buyout would be around $600. If your project included extra pages or features, the total adjusts accordingly.

  • If you cancel without buying out, the site goes offline. Think of the monthly plan like a lease — you can lease forever, lease to own, or walk away. If you do buy out before canceling, everything is yours to keep.

  • Nope! I cover the cost of working with my designer — that's on me. I partner with a professional UX/UI designer because I want every site to feel intentional and polished, and I don't think you should have to pay extra for that. The prices you see on my pricing page include everything: design and development.

  • I handle all payments through HoneyBook, which makes everything simple and secure. You can pay with any major credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover) or ACH bank transfer. Everything is processed securely through Stripe on the backend, so your payment info is always protected.

  • For lump sum projects, yes. A deposit is required to reserve your spot and begin work. My minimum deposit is $100, and for most projects it is 50 percent of the total quote. For the monthly plan, your first month's payment kicks things off.

  • No. I do not use templates or website builders like Wix or WordPress. Everything I build is hand coded from scratch, so your site feels like your brand and not a copy and paste of someone else’s.

  • Custom code is lean, fast, and built only with what your website actually needs. Website builders often come with extra features and plugins running in the background that you may never use, which can slow things down.

    It can also be more secure. With a hand coded site, there are fewer moving parts, fewer third party plugins, and no admin dashboard for someone to try to break into. In other words, there’s simply less stuff for hackers to mess with. Clean, simple, and a lot harder to poke at.

    And of course, custom code gives you more flexibility and uniqueness. Your site is built around your brand, not boxed into a template. Basically, no extra fluff. Just the good stuff.

  • For lump sum projects, if you need to cancel after we've started, you can — no hard feelings. Your deposit is non-refundable since it reserves your spot and covers time invested. Any remaining balance for unfinished work gets refunded.

    For the monthly plan, you can cancel after your 6-month minimum. If you cancel without buying out, the site goes offline. If you want to keep it, you can buy out at any time and your monthly payments get subtracted from the total.
