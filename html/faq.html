  • You're not getting files to install yourself, you're getting my service. Every template is hand-coded by me, so when you purchase one, I take the base design and tailor it to your brand by swapping in your colors, font style, and images. The structural layout stays the same, but the site becomes yours.

  • Starter ($250) is a streamlined one-page site — great if you're just getting online. Standard ($500) is a multi-page site with more room to showcase your work and services. Premium ($850) is built for stylists ready to get serious about their brand — shops, waitlists, deeper customization, and the most room to scale. Each tier includes 10 unique template designs to choose from.

  • You won't be editing anything yourself. I handle all of it. You'll send me your branding (colors, fonts, photos, service list, etc.) and I'll apply it to the template for you. That's the whole point of this service: you get a professional, custom-looking site without touching any code.

  • Not at all. This isn't a traditional template where you download files and make changes yourself. I do all the work, you just send me your content and I bring the site to life for you.

  • Once I have your content, it usually takes about 3 to 4 days for me to complete the setup. You'll also need a free GitHub account for the code and a Netlify account for hosting. Netlify hosting typically runs $10–$20/mo depending on your traffic. If you'd rather have me handle hosting for you, I offer a $25/mo hosting service.

  • Because templates are digital files delivered instantly, all sales are final. If you're unsure whether a template is right for you, reach out before purchasing and I'll help you figure out the best fit.

  • I handle the build and development. For visual design, I collaborate with a professional UX/UI designer so every page feels intentional and polished.

  • No, I'm your main point of contact. I'll pass your feedback to the designer and keep everything moving smoothly on your end.

  • Yes. If you have brands or websites you draw inspiration from, share them during onboarding and I'll loop my designer in.

  • Timelines depend on scope and how quickly you send content and feedback. Once I have both, things move fast. You'll always get an estimated timeline up front.

  • Most updates happen over email, with text or a quick call for anything urgent. You'll get progress updates at key milestones so you always know where the project stands.

  • Lump Sum: pay upfront, own everything day one. Monthly: lease the site at $150/mo with a 6-month minimum, then month-to-month with the option to buy out whenever you're ready. Both include design, development, and up to 5 pages.

  • Your buyout is the equivalent lump sum cost minus what you've already paid. Example: if the lump sum would've been $1,500 and you've paid $900 over 6 months, your buyout is around $600. Extra pages or features adjust the total.

  • If you cancel without buying out, the site goes offline. Think of it like a lease. Lease forever, lease to own, or walk away. Buying out before canceling means everything is yours to keep.

  • Builds with 5 pages or less will not incur additional design costs. Anything over 5 will have a design fee applied. For e-commerce sites, the design fee is included in the overall quote due to the complexity of the design and development process.

  • For lump sum projects, yes. The deposit reserves your spot and is typically 50% of the total quote ($100 minimum). For monthly plans, your first month's payment kicks things off.

    For Subscription plans, a one-time setup fee is required to initiate the service.

  • Custom code is lean, fast, and built only with what your site actually needs. It's more secure (fewer plugins, no admin dashboard to break into) and more flexible. Your site is built around your brand instead of boxed into a template.

  • Yes. Every site is fully responsive and tested across phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops to make sure it looks clean and works smoothly everywhere.

  • Lump sum clients: yes, absolutely. You own all the code and files. Monthly plan clients: you'd need to buy out the site first to get full ownership. Once you do, you're free to take it anywhere.

  • Lump sum: your deposit is non-refundable, but any remaining balance for unfinished work is refunded. Monthly: you can cancel after the 6-month minimum. Without a buyout, the site goes offline.

  • Deposits are non-refundable once work begins. After that, payments are tied to progress. You only pay for work that's been completed. Third-party costs like domain and hosting are non-refundable.

  • Since your site is hand-coded, there's no dashboard to click through unless you know how to code. Monthly plan clients get unlimited edits while subscribed. Lump sum and buyout clients can add my $50/mo maintenance plan, or request one-off edits at a flat rate.

  • Ongoing edits, content updates, bug fixes, and general site care. Think of me as your website's personal mechanic. You drive it, I keep it running smooth. Available to both lump sum and template shop clients.

  • Yes. If you don't need ongoing support, request one-off updates as needed and I'll quote a flat rate based on the change so you always know the cost up front.

  • You'll need a domain (your website name) regardless of plan. I recommend Porkbun. For hosting: monthly plan clients have it included. Everyone else can either manage their own (I recommend Netlify, usually $9–$20/mo) or add my $25/mo hosting service.

  • Monthly plan clients have unlimited edits and 24/7 support built in. Lump sum and template shop clients can add the $50/mo maintenance plan or request one-time updates whenever they need them.
