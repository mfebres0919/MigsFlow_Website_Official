A Domain Name

~$12–15/year

Your website address (yourname.com). I recommend buying through Porkbun or Namecheap. This is a yearly cost paid directly to the domain provider.

A GitHub Account

Free

Only required if you choose the DIY hosting option. GitHub stores your website code so Netlify can deploy it. Free to create at github.com.

Acuity Booking Access

Your existing account

I'll need access to your Acuity account to grab the embed code for your booking widget. If you don't have one yet, you can sign up at acuityscheduling.com.

Your Content

Provided by you

Photos, service list, prices, contact info, and any text you want on the site. The faster you get me your content, the faster you go live.
