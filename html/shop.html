Why a Website?

A booking link isn't a brand

Instagram and Acuity are helpful, but they can only take your business so far. A website helps you look more professional, build trust faster, and give clients one place to view your services, see your work, and book with confidence.

01

Look more established

A polished website signals to clients that you're serious about your craft and your business.

02

Everything in one place

Services, gallery, contact, booking — all on one clean page instead of scattered across apps.

03

Build trust faster

New clients feel more comfortable booking when they can see who you are and what you offer.

Not Sure Which?

Which one is right for you?

A quick checklist to help you self-select. If most of these sound like you, that's probably your tier.

01 Starter is for you if...

  • You're newer in business
  • You mainly use Instagram and Acuity right now
  • You want something simple but professional
  • You need a lower-cost option to get started

02 Standard is for you if...

  • You want clients to know more about you
  • You want to answer questions before they ask
  • You want your business to feel more established
  • You want more than just the basics

03 Premium is for you if...

  • You want your site to feel like a full brand
  • You sell products or want to later
  • You want educational content on your site
  • You want the most complete online presence
