FAQ

Common Questions

Do you only build websites for pool builders?

Yes — pool builders and pool contractors are the niche I've focused on. That means I understand the industry, I know what homeowners are looking for when they land on your site, and I build around the specific journey that turns a visitor into a booked consultation.

How is this different from a generic web design agency?

A generic agency builds a site and hands it off. Everything here is designed with your sales process in mind — how a homeowner finds you, how they evaluate you versus competitors, and what makes them pick up the phone or fill out a form. The site isn't just a brochure. It's a client acquisition system.

What does "built to convert" actually mean?

It means every element — layout, copy, calls to action, forms, photo galleries, and the overall flow — is designed so that visitors don't just browse and leave. They take action. That might be filling out a consultation form, calling you directly, or clicking through to your project portfolio. A beautiful site that doesn't generate leads isn't doing its job.

Will my site work well on phones?

Absolutely. Every site is built mobile-first, meaning it's designed for phones before anything else and then scaled up for desktop. Most homeowners searching for pool builders are on their phones, and your site will be ready for them with a fast, smooth experience.

Why not use Wix or a website template?

Templates are built for everyone, which means they're optimized for no one. Your competitors may already be using the same layouts. A custom-coded site loads faster, ranks better on Google, and is built around how your specific customers make decisions — not a generic layout from a library.

What happens after my site goes live?

Every project includes 30 days of post-launch support at no extra cost. If you want ongoing updates and maintenance beyond that, a $50/month plan keeps your site current as your business grows.
