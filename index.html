FAQ

Have a question?

Everything you need to know before we get started.

How long does a website take to build?

Most projects are completed within 2 to 6 weeks from the time I have everything I need from you. Larger or more complex sites may take a bit longer. I'll always give you a clear timeline before we start so there are no surprises.

What do I need to have ready before we start?

At minimum we need your logo, brand colors, and any photos or content you want on the site. If you don't have everything ready yet that's okay. We can use placeholders and fill things in as we go.

How does pricing work?

Every project is scoped based on what you actually need. We offer a lump sum option for clients who want to pay once and be done, and a monthly plan for those who prefer to spread the cost out. Answer the 7 questions in the quote form and we'll send over your custom quote within 24-48 hours.

Will my site work on phones and tablets?

Yes! Every site we build is mobile first. That means it's designed for phones before anything else, then scaled up for tablets and desktops. Most of your visitors are coming from their phones and your site will be ready for them.

Why not just use Wix or Squarespace?

Wix and Squarespace are fine for getting something up quickly. But they come with slow load times, limited flexibility, and templates your competitors are also using. A custom coded site loads faster, ranks better on Google, and is built specifically around your business instead of a generic layout.

What happens after my site goes live?

We offer 30 days of post launch support included on every project. If you want ongoing updates and maintenance beyond that we offer a $50/monthly plan to keep your site current as your business grows.
