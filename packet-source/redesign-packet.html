Do I have to build my full website with you?

No. The redesign is yours to keep either way. Moving forward is completely up to you.

Will my current website change?

No. Nothing on your live site changes unless you decide to move forward.

What if I don't have great photos or a logo?

That's okay. I'll work with what you have and let you know what would make the biggest difference.

Why only 4 redesigns per week?

Due to the overwhelming support on this special, I'm keeping it to four a week. Every redesign is done by me personally, so a small number means each one gets my full attention.

How does the waitlist work?

Spots go to whoever pays their invoice first, and invoices are held for 48 hours. If this week is full, you're added to the waitlist in order, and I'll always email you to let you know where you fall.
