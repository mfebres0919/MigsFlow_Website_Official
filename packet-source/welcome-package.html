    [1 week max]

07 /faqMIGS FLOW

Good to know

How long does a typical project take?

It can take up to 2–5 weeks, but a lot depends on how quickly you get content to me and give feedback on changes and updates — the longer either takes, the longer the build takes. Larger projects can run 6–12 weeks. You'll get a clear timeline in your proposal, so you're never guessing.

What's the difference between Monthly and Lump Sum?

Monthly is essentially a lease — a more affordable way to get a professional site without paying everything upfront. It's a 6-month minimum, includes hosting and lifetime edits & updates, and you don't own the site outright while subscribed. If you ever want to buy it out, you only pay the remaining balance — so if your site would've been $1,250 as a Lump Sum and you'd already paid 6 months at $125/mo ($750), you'd only owe $500 to own it outright. Lump Sum is the opposite: you pay the full price upfront (with a 50% deposit to start) and own the site outright from day one. Hosting is optional at +$25/mo, and unlimited edits can be added for +$50/mo.

What do you need from me to get started?

Brand assets like your logo, a clear idea of your goals, the content you want on the site, and a quick discovery call so I can get to know you and your business.

What happens after the site launches?

Lump Sum clients get a 15-day grace period where I'm available to help with anything that comes up, plus every file you need so you always have full access to your site. Monthly clients get ongoing monitoring — I'm keeping an eye on your site and available anytime you have questions.

This tends to work best when you're —

  • Ready to invest in a real site, not the cheapest option available
  • Able to give feedback and turn things around in a timely manner — a few days
  • Looking for a long-term web design partner, not just a one-off build
